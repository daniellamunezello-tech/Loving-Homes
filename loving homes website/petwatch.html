Petwatch

Check in on your dog from anywhere with secure live viewing and activity-friendly schedules you can trust.

Stay connected

Petwatch lets you peek into approved play and rest areas during open hours. Streams are encrypted, and we never show sensitive staff-only zones—just the spaces where your pup spends their day.

How it works

Log in through the Loving Homes app or web portal, select your location, and choose your dog’s playroom camera. Bookmark favorite time slots when your pup usually zooms with friends.

On-site safety

Cameras complement—not replace—hands-on supervision. Our Canine Coaches remain in the room, and Petwatch is paused during cleaning, rotations, and private moments.
